## Source-note 机制信号 / Source-Note Mechanism Signals

中文：
- 零曲率基线：A/F 对齐的理想基线，不是事实成功声明。
- 曲率偏差阈值机制：低偏差继续；中等偏差澄清、递归展开或再校准；高偏差停机、中止或返回人类判断。
- 递归曲率认证：sub-AF 或 micro-AF 工作在被视为支持父 AF 前，需要回查父 AF。
- 连接边递归 AF 化：当 B-E 或 C-D 等 AF 节点之间的关键连接不能被假定为低曲率时，该连接本身可能需要 micro-AF 展开。
- 事件中心闭合：A 侧压实与 F 侧反向必要性在事件中心汇合，并满足低曲率稳定偏差；micro-AF 闭合必须可回收进 macro-AF，父 AF 才能被视为闭合。
- 多尺度 AF 封装：macro-AF、sub-AF、micro-AF 可存在于不同尺度，但封装是候选行为，不是强制 canonical 行为。
- 宏观 AF 保真：sub-AF 进展不能替换 macro-AF，也不能让局部成功看似解决更大目标。
- F 有效性与 A/F 对应门：执行应区分 F 是否有效、A 是否对应 F，以及表面目标是否其实是路径假扮 F。
- 输入状态门：用户输入可能是 A0、探索、修正、拒绝、证据或任务，不能默认假定为可执行任务。
- 载体无关 AF：AF 应跨语音、文本、文件、GitHub、工具和模型路线保持目标身份。
- AF 应用栈：将 AF 应用于产品、工具或工作流时，应保持理论层、协议层、应用层和执行层区分。

English:
- Zero-curvature baseline: an ideal baseline for A/F alignment, not a factual claim of success.
- Curvature deviation threshold: low deviation may continue; medium deviation may clarify, recursively expand, or recalibrate; high deviation may halt, abort, or return to human judgment.
- Recursive curvature certification: sub-AF or micro-AF work should be checked against the parent AF before its output is treated as supporting the parent goal.
- Edge-to-AF recursion: a key connection between two AF nodes, such as B-E or C-D, may itself need micro-AF expansion when the connection cannot be assumed as low-curvature.
- Event-center closure: A-side compaction and F-side reverse necessity converge on an event center under low-curvature stable deviation; micro-AF closure must be recoverable into macro-AF before the parent AF is considered closed.
- Multi-scale AF encapsulation: macro-AF, sub-AF, and micro-AF can exist at different scales, but encapsulation is candidate behavior, not mandatory canonical behavior.
- Macro-AF preservation: sub-AF progress must not replace the macro-AF or make local success appear to settle the larger goal.
- F validity and A/F correspondence gate: execution should distinguish whether F is valid, whether A corresponds to F, and whether an apparent goal is actually a path pretending to be F.
- Input-state gate: user input may be raw A0, exploration, correction, refusal, evidence, or task; it should not be assumed to be an executable task by default.
- Carrier-agnostic AF: AF should preserve goal identity across speech, text, files, GitHub, tools, and model routing.
- AF application stack: theory, protocol, application, and execution layers should remain distinct when applying AF to products, tools, or workflows.
