# AI Use Disclosure

Status: governance document / non-spec

## Purpose

This document explains how generative AI tools are used in the Ask First (AF) Framework project. It is a governance disclosure for repository transparency. It is not a canonical AF specification, not a legal opinion, and not a substitute for any university, publisher, funder, or supervisor policy.

## How AI Tools Are Used

Generative AI tools may be used in this project for:

- dialogue-based reflection
- drafting
- structural organization
- repository scaffolding
- review support
- Codex-assisted documentation execution

These uses are assistive. They support thinking, organization, editing, documentation workflow, and repository operations. They do not transfer authorship, originator status, or final responsibility away from Carl (Zulpkar Turxun).

## Authorship and Responsibility

Core theoretical claims, conceptual corrections, research direction, acceptance or rejection decisions, and final responsibility belong to Carl (Zulpkar Turxun).

AI-generated text is treated as draft material unless it is reviewed, revised, and accepted. Draft text, candidate concepts, repository scaffolding, or review suggestions produced with AI assistance do not become canonical AF material by default.

AI tools are not listed as authors because they cannot hold responsibility, approve final claims, guarantee factual integrity, or participate in academic accountability.

## Auditability

The project preserves an auditable process through raw notes, candidate concepts, review traces, commits, and pull requests. These materials help distinguish raw material, candidate development, reviewed repository changes, and canonical specification text.

## Policy Boundary

This document is not a legal opinion. It is not a substitute for university, supervisor, publisher, funder, or institutional policy. Where an external policy applies, that policy should be checked directly.
