## 摘要 / Summary
中文：
F 有效性与 A/F 对应门用于区分两件事：F 是否有效，以及 A 是否真的对应这个 F。

English:
The F validity and A/F correspondence gate separates whether F is valid from whether A actually corresponds to F.

## 候选规则 / Proposed Rule
中文：
在执行把用户表达当作已确定目标之前，协议应检查表面 F 在物理、逻辑和价值上是否有意义，并检查 A 是否对应这个 F，而不是对应一个假扮成 F 的路径。

English:
Before execution treats user expression as a settled target, the protocol should check whether the apparent F is physically, logically, and value-wise meaningful, and whether A corresponds to that F rather than to a path masquerading as F.

## 为什么重要 / Why This Matters
中文：
它保护真实 F 不被失败路径杀死，同时也防止不可能或矛盾的路径被提升为有效目标。

English:
It protects real F from being killed by a failed path, while also preventing impossible or contradictory paths from being promoted into valid goals.

## 这不是什么 / What This Is Not
中文：
这不是声称开放 A/F 无效，不是要求完整形式证明，也不是针对非常规目标的拒绝机制。

English:
This is not a claim that open A/F is invalid, not a demand for full formal proof, and not a refusal mechanism for unusual goals.

## 开放问题 / Open Questions
中文：
最小对应检查是什么？不确定 F 如何被保留而不被过早执行？人类裁决应如何记录？

English:
What is the minimal correspondence check? How should uncertain F be preserved without executing prematurely? How should human judgment be recorded?

## 审查风险 / Review Risks
中文：
把不确定性当成无效；过度管制探索性目标；让 AI 把自己的价值判断冒充为 F 有效性。

English:
Treating uncertainty as invalidity; over-policing exploratory goals; letting the AI impose its own value judgment as F validity.
