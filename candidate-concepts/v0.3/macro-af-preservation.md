## 摘要 / Summary
中文：
宏观 AF 保真是防止 sub-AF 进展替代或解决 macro-AF 的候选保真规则。

English:
Macro-AF preservation is a candidate preservation rule that prevents sub-AF progress from replacing or resolving the macro-AF.

## 候选规则 / Proposed Rule
中文：
sub-AF 的完成、失败或工具反馈，在被视为解决、修改或否定 macro 目标之前，必须先映射回 macro-AF。

English:
Completion, failure, or tool feedback from a sub-AF must be mapped back to the macro-AF before it is treated as resolving, modifying, or invalidating the macro goal.

## 为什么重要 / Why This Matters
中文：
局部成功是最容易制造高质量偏航的方式之一：工件可用，但原始 F 已经被替换。

English:
Local success is one of the easiest ways to create high-quality deviation: the artifact works, but the original F has been replaced.

## 这不是什么 / What This Is Not
中文：
这不是抵抗有用子目标，不是否定执行层价值，也不是声称 macro-AF 不可变化。

English:
This is not resistance to useful subgoals, not a denial of execution-layer value, and not a claim that macro-AF is immutable.

## 开放问题 / Open Questions
中文：
sub-AF 完成后，macro-AF 的最小复核是什么？当人类有意改变 macro-F 时，应如何记录？

English:
What is the minimum macro-AF recheck after sub-AF completion? How should macro-F changes be recorded when the human intentionally changes F?

## 审查风险 / Review Risks
中文：
过度冻结 macro 目标；让 sub-AF 工作显得不重要；把保真误解成拒绝适应。

English:
Freezing macro goals too rigidly; making sub-AF work appear unimportant; confusing preservation with refusal to adapt.
