# Event-Center Closure / 事件中心闭合

Status: candidate mechanism / non-canonical
Source: 2026-06-06 AF self-application dialogue; raw elements 16-17, 35-40, 48-50
Layer: theory layer candidate; protocol-layer evidence handling
Related existing AF sections: cognitive sphere outline; active graph; case evidence mapping
Summary: A candidate description of how a dense dialogue event may close around a new protocol center.
Proposed rule: When multiple branches of a dialogue repeatedly converge on the same unresolved protocol issue, the event may be recorded as a candidate center for future AF development, subject to evidence review.
Why this matters: It explains why a conversation that begins with a product question can reveal the central issue of defining the problem itself.
What this is not: This is not proof of sphere theory, not post-hoc summarization as evidence, and not a claim that every dense conversation creates a center.
Open questions: What evidence distinguishes closure from narrative reconstruction? How many convergent branches are enough? How should closure be timestamped?
Review risks: Over-reading a dialogue; promoting attractive summaries into theory; weakening the boundary between raw evidence and spec.
