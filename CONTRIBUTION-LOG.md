## Raw / Candidate / Canonical 区分 / Raw / Candidate / Canonical Distinction

中文：
本项目区分 raw material、candidate material 和 canonical specification text。

- Raw material 可包括 dialogue notes、source-note extractions、case observations、draft fragments 和 unresolved questions。
- Candidate material 可包括 proposed concepts、review scaffolds、non-canonical mechanisms 和 future-version notes。
- Canonical material 需要明确的人类审查、接受，以及仓库层面的提升。

AI-assisted content 不得在没有明确人类审查的情况下成为 canonical。AI 可以辅助结构化、起草、比较或审查材料，但采纳、否决、修正和最终责任仍由 Carl (Zulpkar Turxun) 承担。

English:
The project distinguishes between raw material, candidate material, and canonical specification text.

- Raw material may include dialogue notes, source-note extractions, case observations, draft fragments, and unresolved questions.
- Candidate material may include proposed concepts, review scaffolds, non-canonical mechanisms, and future-version notes.
- Canonical material requires explicit human review, acceptance, and repository-level promotion.

AI-assisted content must not become canonical without explicit human review. AI assistance may help structure, draft, compare, or review material, but acceptance, rejection, correction, and final responsibility remain with Carl (Zulpkar Turxun).

## 过程证据 / Process Evidence

中文：
公开 GitHub commits、pull requests、review traces、source notes、raw material pools 和 candidate concept files 可作为过程证据。它们有助于显示某个主张何时被起草、何时仍为候选，以及某项变更何时被审查或接受。

这些过程记录不会使 AI 成为共同作者。它们记录的是辅助工具、审查边界和项目治理轨迹。

English:
Public GitHub commits, pull requests, review traces, source notes, raw material pools, and candidate concept files serve as process evidence. These records help show when a claim was drafted, when it remained candidate, and when a change was reviewed or accepted.

These process records do not make AI a co-author. They document assistive tooling, review boundaries, and the project's governance trail.
